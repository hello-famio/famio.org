How Famio Works

1

Register Your @famio.org Email

Choose a unique email address ending in @famio.org that represents your family. Make it memorable and easy to share.

2

Choose Forwarding Addresses

Select one or more email addresses where you want messages forwarded. Add mom, dad, kids, grandparents—anyone in your family circle.

3

Start Receiving Messages

Any email sent to your @famio.org address automatically forwards to all your chosen addresses. Everyone stays connected!

Example: smithfamily@famio.org → forwards to mom@gmail.com, dad@gmail.com, and kids@gmail.com
