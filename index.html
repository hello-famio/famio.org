Frequently asked questions

How do I send an email from my family address? +

Famio includes SMTP support, so you can send email from your family address using any email client that supports custom SMTP — Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and others. Your SMTP credentials are shown on your manage page after signup. Emails sent this way include a small "Sent via Famio" footer on the free plan. This can be removed on a paid plan in the future.

How many people can be on a family address? +

Up to 6 members on the free plan. That covers most families — parents, kids, grandparents. Each member needs to confirm their email before they start receiving forwarded messages.

Is it really free? +

Yes — free for early adopters while we're getting started. We plan to keep a free tier permanently. If we ever introduce paid plans for extra features, anyone who signed up early will be looked after.

What happens if someone doesn't confirm? +

They simply won't receive forwarded emails until they do. You can resend the confirmation link from your manage page at any time. Unconfirmed members don't count against your 6-person limit in terms of forwarding — they're just pending.

Can I change who receives emails? +

Yes. Use your manage link (sent to your email when you signed up) to add or remove members at any time. Added members get a confirmation email; removed members stop receiving immediately.

Do you store my emails? +

No. Famio is a pure forwarding service — emails pass through and are delivered to your members' inboxes. We store only what's needed to manage your address: the address name, member emails, and your manage link. We never read or archive message content.

I lost my manage link. How do I get it back? +

Open any old manage link you have and click "Send link" — we'll email a fresh one to your owner address. If you've lost access entirely, email us at hello@famio.org.
